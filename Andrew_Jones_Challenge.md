# The Andrew Jones Challenge (Pentadecathlon)

From https://lnkd.in/p/dmedpE8g

Think you’re a real #supercomputing expert?

Time for my annual pre-SC #HPC community fun game post. How will you score?

How many of the following could you *credibly* talk about on a panel at #SC26?

1. Why does an HPC cluster have separate login nodes and compute nodes?
2. Why was transition to multi-core seen as such a disruptive transition at the time?
3. Why PUE is limited as a metric for environmentally responsible supercomputing?
4. What are the pros and cons of pure MPI vs MPI+X parallel models?
5. What are some other examples of Amdahl’s law effects in HPC other than on parallelisation of compute?
6. What are good and bad practices in acceptance testing for supercomputers?
7. Why and how should #Top500 be used in procurement contexts?
8. What are 5 often forgotten costs in Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) models for supercomputers?
9. What have been top 1 good and 1 bad impact of #AI infrastructure in the traditional HPC community and why?
10. Compare the days when we had Cray, SGI, IBM, Bull, etc all competing for HPC deals with today’s era - what’s different, what’s the same, etc?
11. What are I/O requirements so hard to define for HPC systems?
12. Is “private #cloud” a real thing or just good marketing?
13. Why is it so hard for new chip ideas/products/suppliers to gain market traction?
14. What is the most important training HPC users need and why?
15. What are the top 3 ways HPC sysadmin is different to general IT?

Your Scores Rated:

15 : liar (did you see the caveat of “credibly”??)
10-14 : Fire - either unusually battle hardened across HPC aspects, or spectacular HPC BS gamer, or are an AI model
5-9 : Earth - perfect panel member, covers a range of topics well, but doesn’t pretend to know everything!
0-4 : Water - could be in marketing? (😜) or specialist who knows one area really deeply, or what our HPC community always needs ie fresh talent with new ideas unencumbered by history?

How did you score?
